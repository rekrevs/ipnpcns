# T-0018: Construct a compact finite-window convolution filter

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L
**Phase**: -

## Context

T-0014 exposes the boundary convention, convolution realization, compactness, and
finite-rank approximation properties as separate hypotheses. Section 3.2 claims that
the square-integrable impulse responses under consideration yield Hilbert--Schmidt,
hence compact, operators on a finite observation interval.

## Approach

Following project-control decision PCR-016:

1. Select causal truncation on `[0,T]` and the nonzero unit-step impulse response.
   Its convolution is the Volterra operator
   `(Vx)(t) = ∫ s in 0..t, x(s)`, with inputs zero outside the observation window.
2. Build prefix-indicator vectors in recording-window `L²`; express the pointwise
   integral as their inner product with the input. Prove measurability,
   representative independence, linearity, and the concrete bound `‖V‖ ≤ T` for
   `0 ≤ T`.
3. Regard the prefix-vector path as an `L²` Hilbert-valued kernel and approximate it
   by dense simple `L²` functions. Each indicator-constant kernel induces an explicit
   rank-one operator, finite sums have finite rank, and kernel convergence controls
   operator-norm convergence.
4. Deduce compactness from closure of compact operators, package the finite-rank
   approximation property, and instantiate `ConvolutionHypotheses` with
   `.causalTruncation`.
5. State explicitly that periodic and whole-window zero-extension formulas are
   different operators and are not identified by this construction.

## Obstacles

Mathlib does not expose a ready Hilbert--Schmidt operator abstraction for this
kernel. The proof instead uses Hölder's bounded bilinear `L² × L∞ → L²` lift,
simple-function density, explicit rank-one formulas, and norm-closedness of compact
operators. This proves the needed conclusion without adding an axiom or assuming
compactness.
