**Status**: PENDING | **Size**: L
**Phase**: -

## Context

T-0014 exposes the boundary convention, convolution realization, compactness, and
finite-rank approximation properties as separate hypotheses. Section 3.2 claims that
the square-integrable impulse responses under consideration yield Hilbert--Schmidt,
hence compact, operators on a finite observation interval.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Select one explicit boundary convention faithful to the causal finite-window
  model and define its integral convolution operator on scalar `L²`.
- [ ] Prove well-definedness, linearity, and a concrete operator-norm bound from
  stated kernel hypotheses.
- [ ] Prove compactness directly or through explicit finite-rank approximations.
- [ ] Instantiate T-0014's convolution and compactness interfaces.
- [ ] State precisely which alternative boundary conventions are not covered.
- [ ] Build without `sorry` or custom axioms.
