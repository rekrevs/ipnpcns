# T-0016: Prove rank-deficient projected NNLS convergence

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L
**Phase**: -

## Context

T-0010 proves the paper's deterministic step interval under a positive lower
singular-value bound and proves that strict coefficient contraction is impossible in
a nontrivial prediction kernel. The paper also claims convergence to a minimizer when
the minimizer need not be unique.

## Acceptance Criteria

  `0 < ε < 2 / L` under an explicit upper spectral bound.
- [x] Prove asymptotic regularity and convergence, in finite dimension, to some NNLS
  minimizer when the solution set is nonempty.
- [x] Reuse T-0010's proof that all minimizers have the same prediction.
- [x] Keep coefficient uniqueness conditional on injectivity/full row rank.
- [x] Build without `sorry` or custom axioms.

## Approach

Following project-control decision PCR-014:

1. Work directly in the finite Euclidean coefficient space and use only an upper
   Gram spectral bound together with `0 < ε < 2 / L`.
2. Combine orthant nonexpansiveness with the raw Gram-step energy estimate to obtain
   Fejér descent relative to every fixed point.
3. Telescope the descent inequality to obtain summable prediction errors.
4. Use finite-dimensional bounded-subsequence compactness, continuity of prediction
   energy, and Fejér monotonicity to identify a minimizer cluster limit and prove
   convergence of the whole sequence and then asymptotic regularity.
5. Translate the fixed-point conclusion to NNLS optimality and reuse the established
   common-prediction and conditional-uniqueness theorems.

## Implementation Notes

- Added `IPNPCNS/Learning/NNLSRankDeficient.lean` with an explicit
  `DesignUpperBound` interface and no lower singular-value premise.
- Proved raw-step prediction-energy descent, projected Fejér descent, monotonicity of
  the distance to every fixed point, a telescoping partial-sum bound, and convergence
  of prediction energy to zero.
- Used a compact coordinate box around a supplied minimizer to obtain a convergent
  tail subsequence. Its limit is feasible and has the minimizer prediction, hence is
  itself an NNLS minimizer.
- Re-applied Fejér monotonicity to that cluster minimizer to prove convergence of the
  whole coefficient sequence. Asymptotic regularity follows by subtracting the
  convergent sequence from its one-step shift.
- Added witness and set-nonemptiness forms of the result, plus an injective-prediction
  specialization that makes the coefficient limit unique.
- Reused `projectedBatchStep_fixed_iff_nnlsMinimizer` and
  `nnlsMinimizers_prediction_eq`; the optimization algebra is not duplicated.
- The compactness route makes a separate square-summability-of-steps lemma
  unnecessary: summable prediction errors identify a minimizer cluster point, after
  which Fejér monotonicity yields full convergence and asymptotic regularity.
