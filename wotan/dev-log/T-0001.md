# T-0001: Assess Lean formalization feasibility of the CNS model

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L

## Context

Assess how realistic it is to formalize and mechanically verify the definitions,
mathematical model, and proofs in `2309.02332v3.pdf` using Lean. The assessment
must distinguish faithful formalization from theorem checking under strengthened
or clarified assumptions, and it must identify the smallest useful pilot.

## Approach

1. Extract and map the full paper: sections, definitions, propositions, theorems,
   lemmas, proofs, examples, and external mathematical dependencies.
2. Inspect notation-heavy and proof-critical pages visually.
3. Translate the dependency graph into candidate Lean types and library imports,
   marking where the paper needs clarification or stronger hypotheses.
4. Validate assumptions about current Lean/mathlib support against official sources.
5. Produce a decision-oriented feasibility report and a bounded pilot proposal.

## Outcome

The deterministic cone-algebra core is a credible Lean target. A bounded 4–8
person-week pilot should formalize Moreau decomposition and double rejection before
any commitment to the estimated 12–24 person-month broad formalization. Lean can
verify conditional mathematical consequences, but not the empirical adequacy of the
CNS interpretation.
