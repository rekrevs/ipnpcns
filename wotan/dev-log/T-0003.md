# T-0003: Formalize Moreau decomposition and double rejection in Lean

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L

## Context

Verify the paper's central projection identity (98)/(108) for arbitrary closed convex
cones in a real Hilbert space. This is the first implementation gate for the overall
formalization.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Prove uniqueness and the variational and cone-specific characterizations of
  metric projection corresponding to equations (65), (68)–(70).
- [x] Prove Moreau decomposition and the residual identity (67)–(71).
- [x] Define closure-safe conic projection and rejection corresponding to (73)–(74).
- [x] Prove double rejection (98)/(108) with the paper's quantification.
- [x] Include a finite-dimensional regression example.
- [x] Build without `sorry`, custom axioms, or hidden strengthened assumptions.

### Risks

- Closure of images and sums must remain visible in definitions.

## Approach

Build on the paper-facing `ClosedCone`, `polar`, `conicHull`, and
`metricProjection` definitions established in T-0002. First prove projection
uniqueness and the cone residual characterization, then Moreau, then closure-safe
operations, and finally the pointwise and conic-hull forms of double rejection.

## Implementation Notes

- `MetricProjection.lean` now proves the variational uniqueness theorem, polar
  residual membership, projection–residual orthogonality, and equation (70).
- `Moreau.lean` proves equations (67)–(71), uniqueness of a cone–polar orthogonal
  decomposition, and bipolarity (95).
- `Operations.lean` defines projected generator sets, closure-safe conic projection
  and rejection, and proves the residual-hull form of (74).
- `DoubleRejection.lean` proves the pointwise identity (103) before separately
  lifting it through `conicHull` to (98)/(108).
- `Examples.lean` specializes the final theorem to two-dimensional Euclidean space
  as a finite-dimensional type regression.
