# T-0003: Formalize Moreau decomposition and double rejection in Lean

**Status**: READY | **Size**: L

## Context

Verify the paper's central projection identity (98)/(108) for arbitrary closed convex
cones in a real Hilbert space. This is the first implementation gate for the overall
formalization.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Prove uniqueness and the variational and cone-specific characterizations of
  metric projection corresponding to equations (65), (68)–(70).
- [ ] Prove Moreau decomposition and the residual identity (67)–(71).
- [ ] Define closure-safe conic projection and rejection corresponding to (73)–(74).
- [ ] Prove double rejection (98)/(108) with the paper's quantification.
- [ ] Include a finite-dimensional regression example.
- [ ] Build without `sorry`, custom axioms, or hidden strengthened assumptions.

## Verification Plan

### Hard Endpoints

- [ ] `lake build` - all Lean modules compile.
- [ ] `rg -n '\\bsorry\\b|^axiom ' IPNPCNS` returns no proof gaps.
- [ ] `lake env lean IPNPCNS/Cone/DoubleRejection.lean` verifies the headline module.

### Manual Checks

- [ ] Definitions preserve the paper's norm closure and non-positive polar.
- [ ] The final theorem quantifies over arbitrary real Hilbert spaces and closed convex
  cones.
- [ ] Pointwise projection equality is not conflated with equality of conic hulls.

### Risks

- Mathlib has the Hilbert projection existence and variational theorem but no bundled
  Moreau API; prove the missing bridge locally.
- The paper's polar sign is opposite to mathlib's dual-cone convention; isolate this in
  explicit lemmas.
- Closure of images and sums must remain visible in definitions.

## Approach

Build on the paper-facing `ClosedCone`, `polar`, `conicHull`, and
`metricProjection` definitions established in T-0002. First prove projection
uniqueness and the cone residual characterization, then Moreau, then closure-safe
operations, and finally the pointwise and conic-hull forms of double rejection.
