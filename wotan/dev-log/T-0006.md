# T-0006: Derive inner-product distortion and the sparse Gershgorin/RIP bound

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L

proved the projection-error consequence. Two independent algebraic endpoints remain:
derive equation (162) from (161) on a linear subspace, and derive the order-`k`

## Approach

Use the real polarization identity on norm-balanced vectors in the selected subspace:
apply (161) to `‖v‖ • u ± ‖u‖ • v`, bound the two quadratic distortions, and divide by
the positive norm product; handle either zero vector separately. For (171), expose the
finite Gram matrix and prove the bound directly on an explicit active `Finset`. Split
the quadratic error into diagonal and off-diagonal terms, use symmetry and
`2 |xᵢ xⱼ| ≤ xᵢ² + xⱼ²`, and count at most `k - 1` neighbours per active coordinate.
This yields the paper's constant without invoking probabilistic or spectral claims.

## Implementation Notes

- Added a bilinear scaled-inner-error API and proved its diagonal absolute bound
  directly from (161).
- Used polarization on the norm-balanced vectors `‖v‖ • u ± ‖u‖ • v` in a real
  subspace. The parallelogram law and positive cancellation yield (162) with the same
  constant `ρ`; zero vectors are handled separately.
- Added full and finite-active column Gram matrices, the normalized diagonal and
  coherence predicate from (170), active column combinations, and coefficient energy.
- Expanded the active squared norm into its Gram quadratic form and split it into
  diagonal and ordered off-diagonal errors.
- Proved the exact off-diagonal counting lemma with
  `2 |xᵢ xⱼ| ≤ xᵢ² + xⱼ²`, obtaining `card s - 1` rather than a weaker `card s`
  constant.
- Proved (171) first for the exact active-cardinality constant and then for every
  `s.card ≤ k`. Natural-number subtraction and a separate emptiness lemma make the
  `k = 0` behavior explicit.
