# T-0006: Derive inner-product distortion and the sparse Gershgorin/RIP bound

**Status**: READY | **Size**: L

## Context

T-0004 formalized the deterministic near-isometry and inner-distortion interfaces and
proved the projection-error consequence. Two independent algebraic endpoints remain:
derive equation (162) from (161) on a linear subspace, and derive the order-`k`
near-isometry bound (171) from diagonal and coherence bounds using Gershgorin or an
equivalent quadratic-form argument.

  in (170).
- [ ] Prove the quadratic-form/RIP bound (171) for vectors supported on at most `k`
  active columns.
- [ ] Keep random-matrix probability claims out of the deterministic theorem.
- [ ] Build without `sorry` or custom axioms.
