## Approach

- Use the literal finite set of all `p`-element subsets of `Fin n` as one axon's
  choice space. Use the finite Cartesian power over `Fin m` as the global uniform
  sample space, making independence across axons structural rather than axiomatic.
- Count local miss choices by deleting a fixed target and use the binomial identity
  `choose (n - 1) p * n = choose n p * (n - p)`.
- Express occupied cardinality as a sum of target indicators, factor the all-miss
  expectation with the finite-product expectation theorem, and exchange the two
  finite sums to prove (30).
- Keep the exponential replacement behind a named error bound. Treat `q² / n` as an
  exact conditional consequence of separately named uniform-marginal and targetwise
  independence premises.

## Implementation Notes

- Proved that sample membership means every axon chooses exactly `p` distinct targets.

- Proved that the product experiment raises this miss probability to the `m`th power,

  The overlap theorem requires both uniform marginals and targetwise independence.

## Obstacles

The exact experiment samples without replacement within each axon but independently
between axons. This required counting `p`-subsets that avoid a fixed target; treating
all `mp` collaterals as independent draws would have proved a different formula and
was not used.
