## Approach

First prove an exact finite first-order identity for `nnlsLoss`: its increment is the
coordinate pairing with `nnlsGradient` plus one half of the squared prediction
increment. Derive the directional derivative and convex quadratic remainder from this
identity. Next prove complementarity implies global optimality by splitting the linear
term coordinatewise, and prove necessity using feasible one-coordinate positive and
negative perturbations. Finally isolate the update map and prove convergence from an
explicit contraction toward a fixed point; then provide a matrix-level sufficient
contraction theorem if the finite spectral estimate remains bounded. Keep every
stochastic premise outside this deterministic layer.

## Obstacles

No blocking obstacle. The familiar scalar condition `ε < 2 / ‖X‖²` is not by itself a
strict contraction on coefficient space when `Xᵀ` has a kernel. The theorem therefore
uses the exact matrix spectral contraction needed for convergence to a unique fixed
point. T-0010 will derive this interface from explicit finite Gram/singular-value
bounds and record the rank-deficient alternative.
