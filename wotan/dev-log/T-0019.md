# T-0019: Prove cone-projection span reduction and homogeneity

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: M
**Phase**: -

## Context

Section 5.3 states two global structural facts before its piecewise-linear face
discussion. Equation (195) says that projecting onto a finitely generated cone depends
only on the component in the cone's real linear span. Equation (196) says that cone
projection is positively homogeneous. The existing metric-projection layer contains
the variational and Moreau characterizations needed to prove both, while T-0007 only
uses a sufficient active-face certificate for equation (199).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Prove `P_C (a x) = a P_C x` for every closed cone, `0 ≤ a`, and arbitrary
  real Hilbert space.
- [x] Prove that `P_C x = P_C (P_span(C) x)` whenever the cone span has an
  orthogonal projection, with all closure or finite-dimensional premises explicit.
- [x] Show that a finitely generated cone has the same real span as its generators
  and derive equation (195) without finite-dimensionality of the ambient space.
- [x] Connect the new laws to the finite active-face/population layer and update the
  model-obligation boundary.
- [x] Build without `sorry` or custom axioms.

### Risks

- The closed conic hull is a closure of a nonnegative span, whereas equation (195)

- Typeclass inference for orthogonal projection onto a finite-dimensional submodule
  may require packaging the cone-span finite-dimensionality explicitly.

## Approach

Following project-control decision PCR-017:

1. Prove positive homogeneity from the cone--polar--orthogonality characterization of
   metric projection, treating the zero scalar within the same nonnegative theorem.
2. Prove a general span-reduction lemma for any submodule containing the cone and
   admitting orthogonal projection. Use orthogonality of `x - P_S x` to show that the
   variational inequalities against cone points are unchanged.
3. Define the real span of a closed cone and specialize the general lemma under a
   finite-dimensional-span premise.
4. Prove that the span of a finite closed conic hull equals the span of its generators;
   infer finite dimensionality and state the paper-facing equation (195) corollary.
5. Record explicitly that these global laws support but do not replace the separate
   active-face certificate used for equation (199).

## Obstacles

Rewriting directly from equality of submodules through `starProjection` is dependent
on the projection instance and therefore not type-correct in Lean's ordinary rewrite
tactic. The finite-generator corollary instead applies the general containment theorem
directly. This avoids proof-instance transport and states the same mathematical fact.

## Outcome

Equations (195) and (196) are now verified rather than assumed. Projection onto a
closed cone is positively homogeneous and depends only on the input component in the
cone span when that span is finite-dimensional. For the paper's finite population
cones, this finite-dimensionality follows from the finite generator family itself,
without restricting the surrounding signal Hilbert space.
