## Approach

Use mathlib's `ProperCone ℝ H` for arbitrary closed convex cones in a real Hilbert
space. Build the paper's non-positive polar from the inner-product pairing, define
metric projection noncomputably from the Hilbert projection theorem, and keep finite
generation separate from the general Section 4.5 theory.
