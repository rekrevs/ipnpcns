## Approach

- Define exact directional similarity as the real infimum of projection norms over a
  cone's unit directions, and make all semantic theorems require a nonzero source
  cone so the infimum is nonempty.
- Prove firm nonexpansiveness of the project's chosen metric projection directly from
  its two variational inequalities. Use it both for the unit upper bound and the
  finite-frame coverage estimate.
- Represent a finite frame by a finite nonempty index type and nonzero cone members;
  normalize each member explicitly as in (90), avoiding an ambient-space
  `DecidableEq` requirement.
- Prove the proxy is optimistic because every normalized frame ray is an exact unit
  direction. Under two explicit `γ`-coverage premises, prove each directional proxy
  is at most its exact infimum plus `γ`, then combine the two directions through
  `min` to obtain (92).

## Implementation Notes

- Added `IPNPCNS/Cone/Comparison.lean` and proved nonexpansiveness of the custom

- Defined cone unit directions, directed projection-norm infima, symmetric similarity,

- Proved the unnumbered comparison law `A ≤ B ↔ A ¬ B = {0}` and its mutual

- Represented raw finite frames by nonzero cone rays over a finite nonempty index

  finite minima in (91).
- Proved the proxy is optimistic without coverage and proved (92) from two explicit
  `FrameCoverage` assumptions using projection nonexpansiveness.

## Obstacles

The exact infimum need not be attained in an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space. The
coverage proof therefore does not select a minimizer: it shows that proxy minus
`γ` is a lower bound for every exact projection norm and applies the defining
greatest-lower-bound property.
