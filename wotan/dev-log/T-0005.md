## Approach

Split the work into two explicit layers. First formalize a finite-dimensional
nonnegative feasible set, least-squares objective, projected update, and a fixed-point
optimality statement at the level justified by stated hypotheses. Then define a
population-realization interface whose approximation field is exactly equation (204)
and prove only its mathematical consequences (206)–(208). Record stochastic
convergence and empirical adequacy as obligations, not hidden axioms.
