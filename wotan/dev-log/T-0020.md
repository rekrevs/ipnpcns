# T-0020: Derive the membrane update quadratic remainder

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L
**Phase**: -

## Context

T-0014 formalized equation (33) as a stable first-order update plus a supplied
`QuadraticRemainder`. The paper motivates that remainder by assuming feedforward
dynamics are slow over the update interval, but does not state a theorem-level
regularity condition. A Lipschitz drive gives a precise and auditable interpretation:
the exact first-order low-pass response differs from the frozen-input update by a
quadratically small exponentially weighted integral.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Define the exact one-step response of the first-order membrane low-pass equation
  for a complete real normed space using a Bochner interval integral.
- [x] Prove the exponential kernel's constant-drive integral equals
  `1 - exp (-Δt/τ)` for `0 ≤ Δt` and `0 < τ`.
- [x] Decompose the exact response into `firstOrderMembraneUpdate` at the frozen drive
  plus an explicit varying-drive remainder.
- [x] Under a stated Lipschitz bound on the drive over `[0,Δt]`, prove the remainder
  norm is at most an explicit nonnegative constant times `|Δt|²`.
- [x] Construct a `QuadraticRemainder` witness and connect the exact response to
  `membraneUpdateWithRemainder`.
- [x] Keep arbitrary `L²` signals outside the pointwise Lipschitz theorem.
- [x] Build without `sorry` or custom axioms.

## Approach

Following project-control decision PCR-018:

1. Define the scalar causal kernel
   `k(s) = (1/τ) exp (-(Δt-s)/τ)` and prove its positivity and interval integral.
2. Define the exact step as exponential decay of the old state plus the Bochner
   integral of `k(s) • drive(s)` over `s ∈ [0,Δt]`.
3. Add and subtract `drive(0)` under the integral. Use the kernel integral identity
   to recover `firstOrderMembraneUpdate (membraneUpdateFactor Δt τ)` exactly.
4. From `‖drive(s)-drive(0)‖ ≤ L|s|`, kernel positivity, and `exp(-u) ≤ 1` for
   `u ≥ 0`, bound the error by `L * Δt² / (2τ)` or a comparably explicit
   quadratic constant.
5. Package this error as `QuadraticRemainder` and prove equality with the existing
   equation (33) interface.

## Obstacles

The sharper elementary estimate `LΔt²/(2τ)` would require integrating the linear
pointwise bound rather than applying the interval integral's uniform-norm theorem.
The proved `LΔt²/τ` bound has the same rigorous quadratic order, a simpler proof,
and exactly the step-independent constant required by `QuadraticRemainder`.

## Outcome

Equation (33)'s quadratic remainder now has a constructive analytic witness. The
exact first-order low-pass response equals the paper's frozen excitation-minus-
inhibition update plus a remainder bounded by `(L/τ)|Δt|²` whenever the net drive

membership or from biological assumptions.
