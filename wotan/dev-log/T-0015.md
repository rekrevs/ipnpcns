# T-0015: Add Moore-Penrose projector representations

**Status**: READY | **Size**: L
**Phase**: -

## Context

T-0009 proves the intrinsic subspace identities and `Col(A Aᵀ) = Col(A)` without a
pseudoinverse. The paper also spells its projector matrices as `A A⁺`; mathlib 4.31
does not provide that finite Moore--Penrose layer.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Define a finite real Moore--Penrose inverse from an orthogonal decomposition or
  singular-value decomposition, with all four Penrose equations.
- [ ] Prove that `A A⁺` is the orthogonal projector onto `Col(A)` and that `A⁺ A` is
  the projector onto the row space.
- [ ] Recover the paper's pseudoinverse matrix renderings of equations (12), (15)--
  (20), and (22) as corollaries of T-0009's intrinsic theorems.
- [ ] Cover zero, rectangular, and rank-deficient matrices.
- [ ] Build without `sorry` or custom axioms.

## Verification Plan

### Hard Endpoints

- [ ] `lake build` - all Lean modules compile.
- [ ] `rg -n '\\bsorry\\b|^axiom ' IPNPCNS` returns no proof gaps.

### Manual Checks

- [ ] Matrix orientations agree with the paper's row/column conventions.
- [ ] No invertibility or full-rank premise is silently added.

### Risks

- Mathlib exposes finite adjoints and spectral decompositions but no ready
  Moore--Penrose API.
- A reusable SVD construction may dominate the cost relative to the representation
  corollaries.
