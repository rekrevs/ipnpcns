# T-0025: Construct the independent two-message occupancy experiment

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: M
**Phase**: -

## Context

The paper states that two reached-target sets overlap in approximately `q²/n` under
an additional independence approximation. T-0013 preserved this as abstract uniform-
marginal and targetwise-independence premises. The existing finite occupancy sample
space now permits a concrete exact instantiation: sample two messages independently
from the product experiment and average the size of their target-set intersection.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Define the product sample space for two independently sampled finite messages
  and the exact occupied-target overlap count.
- [x] Express overlap cardinality as a sum of products of target-hit indicators.
- [x] Prove that the expected overlap is exactly
  `(exactOccupancyFormula n m p)² / n` for `0 < n` and `p ≤ n`.
- [x] Connect the concrete product experiment to the existing two-message
  independence interpretation without claiming that biological messages follow it.
- [x] Keep degenerate and invalid parameter behavior explicit.
- [x] Build without `sorry` or custom axioms.

## Approach

Use `occupancySamples n m p ×ˢ occupancySamples n m p` as the joint uniform sample
space. Rewrite the intersection count as a targetwise indicator sum, commute finite
expectation with that sum, and factor each target's product expectation using the
Cartesian-product expectation theorem. Substitute T-0013's exact marginal hit
probability and sum the identical target terms.
