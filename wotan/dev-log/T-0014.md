## Approach

- Model a recording window as Lebesgue measure restricted to `[0, T]`, and define
  scalar and finite-vector signals as Bochner `L²` equivalence classes on that
  measure space.
- Lift every bounded spatial linear map pointwise with mathlib's `compLpL`; specialize
  this construction to finite real matrices after upgrading their Euclidean linear
  maps to continuous linear maps.
- Represent temporal filters by continuous linear maps on the signal Hilbert space.
  Prove exact linearity, operator-norm bounds, nonnegative weighted aggregation, and
  preservation of explicitly supplied invariant cones.
- Represent a finite signal dictionary by its algebraic span and prove that this span
  is finite-dimensional. Keep convolution realization, boundary conventions,
  compactness, and Laplace transfer identities in named premise structures rather
  than deriving them from missing kernel regularity.
- Make equation (33) an exact first-order update plus a remainder with a quadratic
  norm bound, and prove the convex-combination stability bound when the membrane
  update factor lies in `[0, 1]`.

  update plus a `QuadraticRemainder`; the membrane factor is proved to lie in
  `[0, 1]` for nonnegative steps and positive time constants.

## Obstacles

Bochner `L²` elements are equivalence classes, so pointwise spatial identities are
necessarily almost-everywhere statements. Also, the image of a closed cone under a
linear map need not be closed; the closed-cone interface therefore uses the closed
conic hull of the exact image. The paper's unspecified boundary convention prevents
deriving a particular convolution operator or its compactness in this task.
