# T-0017: Verify the finite Hann-window wavelet dictionary

**Status**: PENDING | **Size**: L
**Phase**: -

## Context

Section 3.2 gives three explicit Hann-windowed wavelets on a `0.20 s` interval and
claims that they have unit norm, are mutually orthogonal, and have zero mean. T-0014
proved that every finite dictionary has exact finite-dimensional geometry but did not
verify these analytic example calculations.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Define the Hann envelope and the three paper wavelets as elements of the scalar
  recording-window `L²` space.
- [ ] Prove their stated normalization constant, unit norms, pairwise orthogonality,
  and zero means.
- [ ] Recover the exact least-squares and nonnegative examples that use their
  coordinates.
- [ ] Keep baseline subtraction as an explicit modeling convention.
- [ ] Build without `sorry` or custom axioms.
