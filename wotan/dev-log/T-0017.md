# T-0017: Verify the finite Hann-window wavelet dictionary

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L
**Phase**: -

## Context

Section 3.2 gives three explicit Hann-windowed wavelets on a `0.20 s` interval and
claims that they have unit norm, are mutually orthogonal, and have zero mean. T-0014
proved that every finite dictionary has exact finite-dimensional geometry but did not
verify these analytic example calculations.

## Approach

Following project-control decision PCR-015:

1. Fix the paper's exact constants: `T = 0.20 s`, carriers at `30 Hz`, `30 Hz`,
   and `60 Hz`, and normalization `γ = 4 / sqrt (3T)`.
2. Define the zero-extended Hann envelope and wavelet representatives, prove bounded
   measurability, and lift them to `ScalarSignal T`; record equality with the pointwise
   representatives almost everywhere under the restricted recording measure.
3. Prove reusable sine/cosine integral identities for integer Fourier modes on the
   recording interval, expand the Hann products into those modes, and calculate all
   means and Gram entries exactly.
4. Package the three atoms as an orthonormal finite dictionary and instantiate the
   paper's unconstrained, nonnegative, and plasticity examples, including their signed
   residuals.
5. State baseline subtraction as a separate convention rather than identifying the
   wavelets with literal nonnegative firing rates.

## Implementation Notes

- Added `IPNPCNS/Examples/OrthonormalWavelets.lean`. The pointwise envelope is
  `sin²(πt/T)` on the closed interval `[0,T]` and zero outside, with the exact
  rational duration `T = 1/5 = 0.20 s`.
- Represented the 30-Hz cosine, 30-Hz sine, and 60-Hz cosine carriers as Fourier modes
  6, 6, and 12 on the recording interval, and proved public formula theorems reducing
  them to the paper's displayed frequencies.
- Proved bounded measurability and lifted all three representatives into
  `Signal.ScalarSignal T`, retaining an almost-everywhere representative theorem.
- Developed the needed finite Fourier algebra locally: integer-mode integrals,
  product-to-sum identities, Hann-square expansion, diagonal norms, and all three
  cross terms. This yields exact zero means and the identity Gram matrix after
  multiplying by `4 / sqrt(3T)`.
- Defined a two-atom synthesis loss and a global NNLS-minimizer predicate. The three
  paper targets have unique optimal coefficients `(1,2)`, `(1,0)`, and `(1,2)`; the
  latter two have residuals `-2φ2` and `+3φ3` and neuronal outputs `+2φ2` and
  `-3φ3`.
- Defined literal pointwise firing rates separately from their baseline-subtracted
  modulations. No unsupported nonnegativity claim is made for arbitrary baseline and
  amplitude values.

## Obstacles

Mathlib did not package the required Hann-weighted Fourier integrals directly. They
were derived from interval-integral substitution and elementary trigonometric
product identities, without adding assumptions.
