# T-0007: Construct polyhedral active-face projection regions

**Status**: DONE | **Size**: L

## Context

T-0005 isolates equation (199) as a field of `PopulationRegion`. Replace that
conditional field with a constructive finite-dimensional theorem for a finitely
generated cone and an explicitly selected active face.

## Approach

Represent a finitely generated cone by nonnegative coefficients over a finite generator
family, and a selected face by a `Finset` of generator indices. Define its linear span
and the candidate orthogonal projection onto that span. State an active-region
certificate in generator coordinates: the candidate is a nonnegative combination of
selected generators, the residual has nonpositive inner product with every generator,
and candidate/residual are orthogonal. Prove that these finite inequalities imply
membership in the full cone and its polar, then apply the verified Moreau uniqueness
theorem to obtain equation (199). Package the result as a constructor for
`PopulationRegion`, with explicit behavior for empty and boundary active sets.

## Implementation Notes

- Added closed nonpositive inner halfspaces and proved that polar membership for a
  closed conic hull is equivalent to checking its generators.
- Defined a closed finitely generated cone, selected generator span, its orthogonal
  projection, and `ActiveFaceCertificate` with explicit subset, coefficient, primal,
  and residual inequalities.
- Proved that the coefficient expansion places the candidate in the ambient cone and
  that finite residual inequalities place the residual in its polar.
- Used orthogonal-projection geometry and the existing Moreau characterization to
  prove constructive equation (199).
- Defined the certified active region and built
  `PopulationRegion.ofFiniteActiveFace`; its `projection_eq_face` field is discharged
  by the theorem rather than accepted as input.
- Proved that the empty selected set has zero projection, characterized its region by
  polar inequalities, and proved that overlapping boundary certificates necessarily
  return the same projected point.

## Obstacles

Mathlib does not currently expose a ready intrinsic polyhedral-face/relative-interior
API matching the paper's language. The implemented KKT/Moreau certificate is a
constructive sufficient region and is explicitly not claimed to be necessary. This is
enough to remove equation (199) as an assumption from the population interface.
